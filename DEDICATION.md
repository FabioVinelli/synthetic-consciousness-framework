# Dedication

Dedicated to the memory of **Jorge Roberto Teixeira Braga**.

His independent exploration of consciousness helped inspire questions at the foundation of this research. This project honors that intellectual influence and the friendship through which his work became part of Fabio Vinelli Lopes's own exploration.

Braga's original philosophical work stands as his own contribution. The Synthetic Consciousness Framework (SCF), the Conscious Agent Framework Harness (CAFH), and their computational interpretations are later work conceived and developed under Fabio Vinelli Lopes's research program.

This dedication does not attribute SCF/CAFH to Braga or imply that he endorsed later interpretations involving AI.

**Original consciousness work:** Jorge Roberto Teixeira Braga.  
**SCF/CAFH conception, computational adaptation, architecture, and research program:** Fabio Vinelli Lopes.  
**AI-assisted drafting/engineering support:** disclosed in [research provenance](research/README.md).
